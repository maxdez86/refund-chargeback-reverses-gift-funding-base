# S15 - Step 05 - Carrosséis Narrativos e Visuais

## Goal

Specify the carousel sections that tell the couple's story and present pre-wedding imagery.

Read `15-design-references.md` before making decisions in this step. Apply insights about carousel transitions, image treatment, and emotional motion pacing to both sections here.

These sections carry the emotional and visual core of the page. They must feel like swipeable chapters from a premium wedding memory deck.

---

## Scope

This step covers:

- Section 3, `Nossa História`
- Section 4, `Pré-Wedding`
- story card sequence
- media handling
- photo placeholder strategy
- captions
- carousel controls and progress cues for these sections

This step does not cover:

- padrinhos and vendors
- gift registry cards
- RSVP group lookup
- global carousel rules already defined in Step 03

---

## Section 3 - Nossa História

Purpose:

- tell the relationship story through horizontal chapter cards
- make the couple feel personal, warm, and specific
- keep copy refined, clear, and not overly sentimental

Carousel requirement:

- Use a horizontal story carousel.
- Each card tells one chapter with media and text.
- Cards must be understandable independently.
- The sequence must stay in the required order.

Required card sequence:

1. `A aula de teatro`
   - Brida and Max met in theatre class.

2. `Par romântico em cena`
   - They worked together in a play as a couple.

3. `O primeiro beijo`
   - The first kiss happened during a rehearsal.

4. `Depois da cortina`
   - They started going out after the play ended.

5. `Belo Horizonte`
   - The official relationship request happened during a trip to Belo Horizonte for a football game.

6. `Mais de 12 viagens`
   - They have taken more than 12 trips together. This may use one or more cards if the design benefits from it.

7. `O pedido nas alturas`
   - The engagement happened during a hot air balloon flight.

Copy requirements:

- visible copy must be pt-BR
- keep each card concise
- use warm, natural language
- do not invent extra factual events beyond the provided story beats

Recommended card structure:

- chapter number or small label
- title
- image/video slot using the Couple Photo Source Library from Step 01 where a Brida-and-Max photo improves the chapter
- short paragraph
- subtle progress indicator

Recommended pt-BR heading:

- `Nossa História`

Recommended pt-BR intro:

- `Antes do grande dia, existe uma história feita de encontros, palco, viagens e escolhas vividas com carinho.`

## Section 4 - Pré-Wedding

Purpose:

- create visual beauty and emotional connection
- present pre-wedding imagery as a large image-first horizontal carousel

Image handling:

- Use the Couple Photo Source Library from Step 01 as the primary image source for Brida and Max.
- Use those photos for `Pré-Wedding` and other Brida-and-Max couple media slots where they fit the composition.
- If a listed couple photo is unavailable or unsuitable for a specific slot, create a premium replaceable placeholder.
- Placeholders must make image slots obvious and easy to swap later.
- Placeholders should be realistic and wedding-appropriate, not generic grey boxes.
- Do not use the couple photo source library for `Lista de Presentes`, `Padrinhos e Madrinhas`, or vendor cards.

Carousel requirement:

- Use large visual cards.
- Include captions where useful.
- Keep image areas stable with defined aspect ratios.
- Avoid hiding navigation or captions on hover only.

Recommended pt-BR heading:

- `Pré-Wedding`

Recommended pt-BR supporting copy:

- `Alguns registros para guardar a atmosfera desse caminho até o casamento.`

Photo placeholder requirements:

- each placeholder should identify its intended slot, such as `Foto pré-wedding 01`
- replacement should require changing image paths or data entries, not restructuring layout
- avoid fake claims that the placeholder is a real photo

## Section-Specific Carousel Controls

Use the global carousel model from Step 03.

For `Nossa História`:

- show card number or chapter progress
- make story titles visible at all times
- support horizontal swipe on mobile
- support desktop arrows and keyboard navigation where feasible

For `Pré-Wedding`:

- prioritize image scale
- keep captions readable
- show progress without covering important image areas
- avoid excessive parallax that competes with the photos

Apply carousel transition techniques from `15-design-references.md` that feel premium and emotionally appropriate for wedding storytelling.
