## Research Instructions

For each URL in the list above, perform the following analysis:

### Visual Design

- What is the dominant color palette? List primary, secondary, and accent colors with approximate values.
- What typographic scale and font choices are evident? Note heading weights, tracking, and hierarchy.
- How is whitespace used? Is it generous, tight, editorial, or structured?
- What layout grid or composition pattern is used? Describe the rhythm.
- Are there notable asymmetric or editorial layout moments?

### Animation and Motion

- What types of animations are present? (scroll-triggered reveals, parallax, cursor-reactive, SVG morphing, page transitions, etc.)
- How is motion paced? Is it slow and cinematic, fast and energetic, or somewhere between?
- Are animations decorative or functional? How do they support the narrative?
- What triggers interactions? (scroll position, hover, click, load)
- Is `prefers-reduced-motion` or any graceful degradation visible?

### Carousel and Horizontal Scroll

- Does the site use any carousel, horizontal scroll, or swipe interaction?
- How is the carousel indicated to the user? (arrows, dots, drag hints, partial card peek)
- How are transitions handled between cards?
- Note any particularly elegant or novel carousel technique.

### Emotional Tone

- What emotion does the site evoke on first impression?
- What design choices most directly contribute to that emotion?
- Is there warmth, coldness, tension, joy, intimacy, grandeur?

### Technical Signals

- What stack signals are visible? (CSS custom properties, SVG filters, WebGL, canvas, clip paths, view transitions)
- Are there any interaction patterns that feel particularly well-suited to a mobile wedding experience?

## Usage by Subsequent Steps

Every step from Step 03 onwards must read `15-design-references.md` before making implementation decisions. If all steps are happening in one continuous Bolt session, keep this document updated and continue using it as the shared reference.

The Distilled Creative Brief section of that file is the primary reference. Implementation steps should reflect the research findings in their design choices, specifically for:

- motion choreography and animation technique (Step 03 and 04)
- carousel behavior and progress cues (Step 03 and later)
- hero composition and first-impression impact (Step 04)
- overall visual rhythm and typography scale (all steps)

If the research findings conflict with a hard rule from Step 01 (such as pt-BR copy, mobile-first usability, or anti-generic constraints), Step 01 takes precedence.
