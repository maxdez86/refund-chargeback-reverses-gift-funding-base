# S15 - Step 03 - Estrutura Geral da Página e Navegação

## Goal

Specify the single-page architecture and global interaction rules that every later section must use.

Read `15-design-references.md` before making decisions in this step. The Distilled Creative Brief in that file must inform carousel behavior, motion pacing, and layout rhythm choices.

This step defines the page shell, navigation, carousel behavior, accessibility expectations, and responsive behavior. It does not define the internal content of each section.

---

## Scope

This step covers:

- vertical page rhythm
- section order
- persistent or easily reachable RSVP affordance
- smooth scrolling
- global carousel interaction model
- accessibility and keyboard expectations
- reduced-motion behavior
- responsive constraints
- event-detail visibility

This step does not cover:

- individual card copy
- guest lookup data

- gift item inventory

## Global Carousel Model

The core interaction pattern:

- The visitor scrolls vertically through the page.
- Long content sections become horizontal swipe experiences.
- Each carousel feels like opening memory cards, not like browsing a generic product carousel.

Required carousel behavior:

- horizontal swipe or drag on touch devices
- arrow controls on desktop
- keyboard navigation where feasible
- trackpad horizontal scrolling where feasible
- scroll snapping or equivalent settling behavior
- visible progress using dots, a rail, card numbers, or another clear indicator
- card titles and primary actions visible without hover
- first card may partially hint at the next card where helpful
- vertical scrolling must not be trapped
- horizontal movement must feel controlled, not slippery or confusing
- `prefers-reduced-motion` must be respected

Carousel sections:

- `Nossa História`: narrative chapter cards with media and text
- `Pré-Wedding`: large image-first cards
- `Padrinhos e Madrinhas`: grouped people cards
- `Fornecedores`: vendor cards
- `Lista de Presentes`: gift item cards

Apply insights from the design reference research in `15-design-references.md` to the carousel transitions, drag behavior, and progress indicator design.

## Responsive Requirements

Mobile:

- prioritize WhatsApp entry and fast comprehension
- use comfortable touch targets
- make carousel affordances obvious
- avoid tiny text in card bodies
- avoid hiding critical event information in horizontal-only content
- keep form controls full-width or easy to tap

Desktop:

- use the extra width for editorial composition, not clutter
- support arrows, keyboard movement, and trackpad scrolling for carousels
- preserve strong visual rhythm and whitespace

All viewports:

- text must not overlap other UI
- buttons and labels must fit their containers
- cards must have stable dimensions
- image placeholders must have stable aspect ratios
- couple imagery must follow the Couple Photo Source Library rules from Step 01

---

## Accessibility

Use:

- semantic HTML where possible
- labels for form controls, not placeholders alone
- accessible names for icon-only controls
- visible focus states
- keyboard-reachable carousel controls
- sufficient contrast
- `aria-live` or equivalent only for meaningful dynamic RSVP feedback

Avoid:

- hover-only information
- keyboard traps
- carousel controls without labels
- motion that blocks task completion

---

## Motion Rules

Motion should support pacing, emotion, and horizontal exploration.

Use the motion techniques identified in `15-design-references.md` where they improve storytelling or emotional impact.

Use:

- gentle section reveals
- controlled card transitions
- progress feedback
- subtle parallax only where it improves storytelling

Avoid:

- exhausting animation
- scroll hijacking
- delayed content required for comprehension
- motion during RSVP that makes the form harder to complete

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing nonessential transitions and keeping carousel navigation functional.
