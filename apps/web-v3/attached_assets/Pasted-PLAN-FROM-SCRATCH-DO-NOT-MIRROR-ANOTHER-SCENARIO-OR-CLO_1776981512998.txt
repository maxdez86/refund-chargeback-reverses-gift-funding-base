# S15 - Step 04 - Hero, Contagem Regressiva e Local

## Goal

Specify the first conversion path and the essential event information.

Read `15-design-references.md` before making decisions in this step. The Distilled Creative Brief must inform hero composition, animation technique, and first-impression choices.

This step covers the opening frame, the countdown, and the venue section. These sections must make the wedding identity, date, time, and location immediately clear for mobile guests.

---

## Scope

This step covers:

- Section 1, `Hero`
- Section 2, `Countdown`
- Section 5, `Local da Cerimônia`
- first-viewport CTA placement
- event-detail presentation
- map/link affordance

This step does not cover:

- story carousel
- pre-wedding carousel
- RSVP group lookup internals
- gift list content

---

## Section 1 - Hero

Purpose:

- establish the premium carousel-led wedding identity immediately
- make `Brida e Max` the dominant first-viewport signal
- give guests the essential event details
- provide the primary RSVP action

Required visible content:

- `Brida e Max`
- `06/12/2026`
- `15:00`
- `Buffet Tulipas - Unidade Villa Valentim`
- `Confirmar Presença`
- optional secondary link to `Lista de Presentes`

Hero treatment:

- free-style opening frame as defined in Step 01
- editorial, warm, playful-premium composition
- may use cropped photo moments from the Couple Photo Source Library defined in Step 01
- use premium replaceable image placeholders only if the listed couple photos are unavailable or unsuitable for the chosen composition
- may include a subtle carousel cue that previews swipeable chapters
- must not look like a generic centered wedding template
- apply hero composition and first-impression insights from `15-design-references.md`

Recommended pt-BR supporting copy:

- `Vamos celebrar esse dia ao lado das pessoas que fazem parte da nossa história.`
- `São Paulo, 06 de dezembro de 2026`

Implementation requirements:

- primary CTA scrolls to `#confirmar-presenca`
- secondary CTA scrolls to `#presentes`
- event details remain readable on mobile
- image treatment must not obscure text
- do not use the Couple Photo Source Library for gift, padrinhos, or vendor imagery
- do not depend on hover for any important information

---

## Section 2 - Countdown

Purpose:

- create anticipation for the ceremony
- reinforce the date and time
- keep the page emotionally warm without reducing clarity

Countdown target:

- `06/12/2026 15:00`
- local wedding time in São Paulo, Brazil

Required display units:

- dias
- horas
- minutos
- segundos

Recommended pt-BR labels:

- `Faltam`
- `dias`
- `horas`
- `minutos`
- `segundos`
- `para celebrarmos juntos`

Behavior:

- update live on the frontend
- keep numbers readable
- handle post-event state gracefully with pt-BR copy such as `Hoje celebramos esse momento especial.`
- avoid over-animated numeric changes

---

## Section 5 - Local da Cerimônia

Purpose:

- make the venue immediately useful for guests
- reduce friction for mobile visitors who need directions

Required visible content:

- `Buffet Tulipas - Unidade Villa Valentim`
- `Rua Valentim Magalhães, 293 - São Paulo - SP`
- `06/12/2026`
- `15:00`

Recommended pt-BR headings and labels:

- `Local da Cerimônia`
- `Buffet Tulipas - Unidade Villa Valentim`
- `Rua Valentim Magalhães, 293 - São Paulo - SP`
- `Ver no mapa`
- `Cerimônia às 15h`

Map/link requirement:

- Include an elegant map or external map link affordance when possible.
- The map action should open directions for the exact address.
- If embedding a map is not practical, use a polished link button instead.

Mobile requirements:

- address must be selectable or easy to copy when feasible
- map action must have a comfortable touch target
- venue information must not be hidden inside a carousel
