## Creative Variables

This scenario is FREE-STYLE. The implementing model decides and justifies all four creative variables. Do not use fixed scenario codes from other experiments.

### Design System

Propose a bespoke editorial carousel system.

Requirements:

- image-led cards
- tactile swipe areas
- visible progress cues
- accessible controls
- clean responsive sections
- intentionally custom feel, not tied to a named or generic design system

State your chosen design system in 2-4 sentences and justify the decision before building.

### Tone

Requirements:

- warm, playful-premium, and emotionally clear
- elegant enough for a wedding invitation
- clear enough for family guests and older visitors arriving from WhatsApp

State your chosen tone in 1-2 sentences before building.

### Motion

Requirements:

- carousel-native motion defines the interaction language
- horizontal snap scrolling
- soft drag/swipe behavior
- card reveal sequencing
- progress indicators
- subtle parallax only where it genuinely improves storytelling
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout
- motion must never block reading, event comprehension, or RSVP completion

State your motion approach in 1-2 sentences before building.

### Hero

Requirements:

- free-style opening frame
- `Brida e Max` must be the dominant first-viewport signal
- may combine typography, cropped photo moments from the Couple Photo Source Library, carousel hints, and event details
- do not force a typography-first-only composition
- do not use a basic centered couple photo with a button stack unless it is heavily art-directed

State your hero concept in 2-4 sentences before building.

## Anti-Generic Rules

Avoid:

- startup SaaS look
- generic AI landing page templates
- tacky wedding cliches
- childish romance
- decorative overload
- wedding clipart
- fake testimonials
- clutter
- too much pink
- noisy gradients
- cheap visual effects
- over-animation
- placeholder English
- unfinished buttons that imply real backend behavior
- a basic centered hero with a generic couple photo and button stack unless it is heavily art-directed

Prefer:

- editorial luxury
- confident whitespace
- strong typography
- subtle warmth
- premium rhythm
- modern sophistication
- timeless elegance
- clear mobile conversion
- real content used with intention
- bespoke composition instead of template symmetry
